Frequently asked questions

Not sure where to start? Get in touch and tell us a little about the work you have in mind.

Do you take on domestic and commercial work?

Yes — Fenland Plastering Services is presented here for both domestic and commercial enquiries. Please contact us to discuss the size and requirements of your project.

Can I get a quote for my project?

Contact us with a brief description of the work and, if possible, a few photographs. We can then discuss the next steps.

What areas do you cover?

Please contact us to confirm whether your property is within our working area.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Your location, the type of work required, approximate room or project size and any useful photos are a good place to start.
